# CloudeAlam Pricing

CloudeAlam uses monthly pricing for most infrastructure services.

Prices in this document are listed in EUR and do not include applicable taxes.

## VPS

| Plan | vCPU | RAM | Storage | Traffic | Monthly Price |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic | 2 | 4 GB | 80 GB | 2 TB | €8 |
| Pro | 4 | 8 GB | 160 GB | 5 TB | €16 |
| Business | 8 | 16 GB | 320 GB | 10 TB | €32 |

## Managed Database

| Plan | vCPU | RAM | Storage | Monthly Price |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Starter | 2 | 4 GB | 50 GB | €18 |
| Standard | 4 | 8 GB | 150 GB | €42 |
| Pro | 8 | 16 GB | 300 GB | €85 |

## Object Storage

Standard storage costs €0.02 per GB/month.

Infrequent Access storage costs €0.012 per GB/month.

Network transfer charges may apply to data downloaded from Object Storage.

## Additional IPv4 Address

An additional public IPv4 address costs €2/month.

A public IPv4 address included with a VPS is not charged separately.

## Backups

VPS automated backups are charged separately from the VPS instance.

Backup storage costs €0.01 per GB/month.

## Billing

Customers are billed monthly.

VPS and database resources are billed based on the selected plan.

Additional usage charges are calculated separately when applicable.

Customers can view current usage and estimated monthly costs from the CloudeAlam dashboard.
