# CloudeAlam Support

CloudeAlam provides technical support for customers using CloudeAlam infrastructure services.

## Support Channels

Customers can contact support through:

- Support tickets
- Email
- Enterprise emergency channel

## Standard Support

Standard support is available 24/7.

The target initial response time is:

- Critical incidents: within 2 hours
- High priority issues: within 6 hours
- Normal issues: within 24 hours

Response time is a target and not a guarantee unless explicitly included in the customer's SLA.

## Enterprise Support

Enterprise customers receive priority support.

Enterprise support includes:

- 24/7 priority support
- Emergency incident channel
- Dedicated escalation process
- SLA-based response targets

## Support Priorities

### Critical

A critical issue causes a complete production outage or makes a major production service unavailable.

### High

A high-priority issue significantly affects production functionality but does not completely stop the service.

### Normal

Normal issues include configuration questions, documentation questions, and non-critical technical problems.

## What Support Does Not Cover

CloudeAlam support does not normally include:

- Application code debugging
- Custom software development
- Customer-specific architecture implementation
- Managing third-party services

Support engineers may provide general guidance when appropriate.
