# CloudeAlam Service Level Agreement

This SLA defines the service availability commitments provided by CloudeAlam to eligible customers.

## Standard Customers

Standard VPS customers receive a target monthly availability of 99.9%.

The standard SLA does not provide service credits for availability below the target.

## Enterprise Customers

Enterprise customers may receive a 99.99% monthly availability commitment depending on their contract.

Enterprise SLA terms are defined in the customer's service agreement.

## Availability Calculation

Monthly availability is calculated based on the total number of minutes in the calendar month minus eligible downtime, divided by the total number of minutes in the month.

Scheduled maintenance announced in advance is excluded from eligible downtime.

## Service Credits

Enterprise customers may receive service credits when the availability commitment defined in their contract is not met.

The amount of the service credit depends on the availability level achieved during the billing month.

## Service Outages

If a CloudeAlam service becomes unavailable, the operations team investigates the incident and works to restore service as quickly as possible.

Customers can monitor active incidents through the CloudeAlam status page.

## SLA Limitations

The SLA does not cover downtime caused by:

- Customer configuration
- Customer application errors
- Abuse or prohibited activity
- Third-party services outside CloudeAlam's control
- Force majeure events
- Scheduled maintenance

Enterprise customers should refer to their individual contract for the exact SLA terms applicable to their account.
